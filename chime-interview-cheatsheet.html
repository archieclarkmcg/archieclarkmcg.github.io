  • IP:

  • Your picks:

    • Made In Abyss: style you aspire to make with them.
    • Also: Ni no Kuni, Zelda (new Ocarina of Time).
    • Film: Grave of the Fireflies. Show: Hikaru ga Shinda Natsu.

Your introduction

Tokyo and London. Want more time on 3D art, with planners and programmers alongside.

  1. Now: Technical Artist, Feral Interactive (London). Only technical artist on porting projects. Matching existing style, optimising across devices, daily talks with design and engineering.
  2. Tokyo: about a year as technical art intern at Meta Meow, alongside outsourcing teams on AAA projects. Shipped three tools (docs, support, updates). Shaders, materials, VFX.
  3. Sporty Group: rigging, weight painting, animation of stylised sports characters in Blender. Edited models to work in game.
  4. Passion: Digital Umami, freelance.
  5. Why Chime: established games company; love Japanese culture and art; want a more creative role in a team fighting to make a great game. Current job: more problem solving, less collaboration on ideas.

Likely questions

Work, then the move to Japan, then the gaps.

  1. Introduce yourself. Five beats above.
  2. Why Japan? How would the move work? Lived in Tokyo. Needs visa sponsorship (working holiday visa if they prefer). Two months' notice; prep and onboarding while the visa processes.
  3. Why Chime? Art-focused Unreal work, small core team, planners building with tools, in-house well-known IP. Name an IP: Re:Zero or Made In Abyss.
  4. Why more art, less technical art? More idea collaboration, more 3D art; technical skills still help the pipeline.
  5. Walk me through a piece. Pick the Sporty Group character or Hanging Village: what you built, tools, topology, rig and weights, one thing to improve.
  6. Tell me about the university character. Old work. Know the flaws and the fix.
  7. Can you make assets from scratch, in an anime or non-realistic style? Yes: Animal Mystery Crew, Hanging Village, university character. Some isn't on your portfolio.
  8. Give an example of working with planners or programmers. Designers: cut specs to fit time. Programmers: animation to their logic, in-engine scripting. Graphics: optimisation, foveated rendering mask. Have one case where a technical limit changed the art.
  9. What have you done in Unreal? Professionally: tools (editor Blueprints and beyond), game development, environment art. Plus university. Feral is proprietary.
  10. The five years. See five-year gap.
  11. Your strengths and weaknesses. Strength: ownership, clear communication. Weakness: art practice is in spare time; improving daily.
  12. Which games or IP do you love? Re:Zero (chibi 3D + 2D blend, like Persona 5 Tactica); Made In Abyss as the style you aspire to. Also Ni no Kuni, Zelda, Grave of the Fireflies, Hikaru ga Shinda Natsu. Reasons in your own words.
  13. Start date and salary. Two months after accepting; salary: ask their range first.
